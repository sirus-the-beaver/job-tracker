# Job Tracker

## 📌 Overview
The **Job Tracker** is a web application designed for prospective programmers and software engineers to efficiently manage their internship or job search. With the number of Computer Science students in the US quadrupling between 2005 and 2023 [1], coupled with an increasingly competitive job market, it's never been more critical to stay organized and strategic in the application process.

Our platform centralizes all job applications, tracks statuses, manages networking contacts, and provides skill analysis—empowering applicants to make informed decisions and better prepare for interviews.

---

## 🎯 Motivation
The tech job market is becoming increasingly competitive due to:
- A **surge in CS graduates** entering the workforce
- **Hiring slowdowns** in the tech industry
- **Multiple job application platforms** (LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Indeed, Handshake) causing scattered tracking
- **Psychological pressure** on students and recent graduates to secure positions quickly

**The Problem:**
Applicants often juggle dozens—or even hundreds—of applications across multiple platforms. Tracking statuses, following up, and preparing for interviews can become overwhelming. Traditional methods like spreadsheets or notes are time-consuming, lack advanced features, and fail to provide deeper insights.

**The Solution:**
Our Job Tracker streamlines the process by:
- Centralizing application records
- Tracking skill requirements and preparedness
- Organizing networking contacts
- Providing skill analytics to guide professional growth

---

## 🚀 Features
- **🔐 User Authentication**
  - Secure sign-up/login to protect personal data

- **📂 Application Tracking**
  - Store company details, role requirements, application statuses, and networking contacts

- **🛠 Skill Management**
  - Create skills related to jobs/internships applied to  
  - Track which jobs require specific skills  
  - View skill frequency across applications  
  - Log comfort levels to identify areas for improvement

- **🤝 Contact Management**
  - Save contact information for recruiters, hiring managers, or networking connections

- **📊 Insights & Analytics**
  - Real-time skill statistics

---

## 📈 Benefits
- **Efficiency** – Centralized tracking saves time  
- **Preparedness** – Skill tracking and analytics boost interview readiness  
- **Informed Decisions** – Access hiring process insights before applying  
- **Organization** – Avoid missed deadlines or lost opportunities

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React.js  
- **Backend:** Node.js / Express.js  
- **Database:** MySQL  
- **Authentication:** JWT-based secure login system
